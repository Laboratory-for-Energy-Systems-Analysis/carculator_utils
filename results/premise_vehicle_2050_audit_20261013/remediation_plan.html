Car and truck inventory remediation plan

Prepared 13 October 2026 from the 2020/2050 audit and its verification summary. This is an implementation plan; the corrections below have not been applied by writing this document. Work proceeds one issue at a time, with a reviewable change and focused checks before moving to the next. A successful database import is not scientific sign-off.

Scope and decisions

The immediate scope is the installed passenger-car and Regional-delivery truck updates, their shared carculator machinery, and Premise transformations. Buses and two-wheelers still use their original workbooks. Shared-code changes must be checked against those models, but refreshing their inventories is a subsequent exercise.

Keep the accepted 2020 model baseline, Swiss climate, 2020 emission classes, default usage assumptions, the selected car catalogue and all 52 truck service identities. Trucks use Regional delivery and a 400 km design range. Preserve the approved energy-preserving gasoline/LPG proxies, Premise battery-capacity markets, and the companion noise audit. Retain the car cold-start assumption of 1.8 starts/day and the agreed HBEFA provenance wording. Preserve existing service/production names, products, locations and units; do not reinstate the historical car classes removed under the agreed scope.

Two additional decisions were confirmed for this plan:

  • Future vehicles retain their baseline range through consistent resizing. This means 400 km for the selected trucks and the relevant baseline range for each car. Batteries, mass and affected inventories must change together.

  • LPG retains a documented fossil supply where the IAM has no LPG pathway. Do not substitute a petrol or CNG scenario blend implicitly.

Premise continues to own scenario energy supply and efficiency projections. The future resizing implementation must assign each improvement once and distinguish physical battery assumptions from the upstream battery-market mix. Any new unsupported assumption about hybrid architecture, battery technology or fuel carbon origin must be resolved from evidence or brought back for review.

2. Make blend energy and carbon properties explicit

Accounting gap requiring verification. Current adapters preserve energy using reference-fuel properties; the delivered market can contain fuels with different heating values and carbon contents.

Trace the final supplier mix and derive delivered heating value, carbon content and carbon origin on explicit mass, volume or energy bases. Adjust purchased blend quantity to preserve the intended operating energy. Keep compression electricity separate from fuel chemical energy. Document how biogenic carbon, direct-air-capture carbon and recycled fossil carbon are represented; an electrofuel label alone does not determine carbon origin.

Acceptance: independently calculated examples for pure fossil fuel, pure biofuel and mixed petrol/diesel/gas markets preserve energy and yield the expected carbon amounts. Unknown properties remain visible diagnostics. Fuel-mass shares are not used directly as carbon shares. Document the full-oxidation CO2 convention and any separate CO/CH4/NMVOC treatment; do not claim elemental carbon closure without checking those flows.

Main code: Premise fuel-market properties, carbon helpers, and the two vehicle generation adapters. This supplies the accounting contract for step 3.

3. Recalculate fossil/non-fossil CO2 after final vehicle linking

Confirmed defect. Regional clones inherit the EUR template's split even when their actual fuel blend differs. The audit found 348 resolved mismatches; unresolved gas/synthetic routes make this a lower bound.

Calculate tailpipe CO2 from the final linked supplier and final fuel quantity, after regionalization and efficiency adjustment. Use the carbon properties from step 2. Update the reclassification bookkeeping so scaling and repeated updates cannot double-transfer CO2 or reuse a stale transfer amount.

Acceptance: all mapped vehicle regions pass in 2020 and 2050, including non-European cases, pure fossil/pure non-fossil boundaries and mixed markets. Transfers preserve total CO2 when only its classification changes; changes in total CO2 caused by changed blend properties are explained separately. Both CO2 flows remain nonnegative, and rerunning the transformation is idempotent. Check PHEVs and forwarding activities as well as single-fuel vehicles.

Main code: premise/premise/fuels/carbon.py, liquid/gas fuel transformations, premise/premise/transport.py and transformation sequencing.
