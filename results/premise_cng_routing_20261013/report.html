Regional CNG routing: correction and verification

Step 1 of the vehicle remediation plan is complete. Premise now regionalizes vehicle-grade compression before its market. Regional CNG consumers reach regional high-pressure gas and compression electricity, with the source inventory's physical quantities preserved.

What changed

Previously the path was vehicle -> regional CNG market -> RoW compression -> World gas/electricity. The corrected path is vehicle -> regional CNG market -> regional compression -> regional gas/electricity. This applies to all consumers of these suppliers, including cars and trucks.

The original geographic production and market activities now forward exactly one kilogram of the same product to their IAM replacement. In the previous REMIND builds, the original global market forwarded only 12/13 kg: the generic helper divided across thirteen regions, then omitted World from its recipient list. The CNG correction uses the World replacement directly where appropriate. It does not alter the generic helper or unrelated sectors.

Compression retains 1.360544218 m3 of high-pressure gas and 0.361 kWh of medium-voltage electricity per kg of vehicle-grade gas. Infrastructure demand and direct methane/NMVOC/other hydrocarbon losses are retained. These are preserved source coefficients, not a new independent validation of gas density, heating value or carbon content. Those accounting questions are the next task.

Unit tests

Nine new cases in premise/tests/test_vehicle_grade_gas.py cover:

  • The complete market -> compression -> gas/electricity chain in six test regions, including World, for both 2020 and 2050.

  • Preservation of gas-volume conversion, compression electricity, infrastructure and methane losses.

  • One-kg forwarding from CH, RoW and GLO without duplicate compression.

  • Idempotence of repeated regionalization, including the provider index.

  • The public gas-update entry point, so bypassing the helper is detected.

  • Graceful handling of inventories without vehicle-grade gas activities.

The nine cases pass; the combined fuel, transformation and passenger-car integration selection passes 45 tests under Python 3.12. Deliberately restoring the former market-only behavior in a separate process makes six cases fail. Omitting only the forwarding correction makes two cases fail. These checks mutate methods in memory and leave the corrected source intact.

Full scenario checks

Fresh full builds use REMIND SSP2-NPi, ecoinvent 3.12 cutoff, identical encrypted IAM input and the unchanged installed vehicle workbooks. All 13 IAM-region chains pass for both years, covering 78 car and 65 truck CNG links per year. Vehicle operating quantities and direct emissions are unchanged by this fix.

Unit tests and builds use Python 3.12; build records also identify dependency versions, which differ from the earlier Python 3.11 audit environment. Existing saved comparison databases have not been overwritten.
