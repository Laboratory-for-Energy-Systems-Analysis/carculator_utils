Large Car efficiency fallback

The approved REMIND/REMIND-EU fallback now uses the same powertrain's Large Car and SUV relative energy-intensity trend when a region has neither a usable native Large Car intensity nor a usable native energy/service pair. Native Large Car signals take priority. The 2020 vehicle inventories, fleet volumes, technology allocation and all existing supplier mappings are retained. Fuel-tank resizing was separately skipped at the user's request.

Implementation and scope

Five passenger-car entries declare energy_intensity_fallback_aliases for REMIND and REMIND-EU: BEV, FCEV, compressed gas, gasoline PHEV and gasoline. The same-region proxy must have MJ/vehicle-km units and a finite positive 2020 value. Its efficiency index is intensity(2020) / intensity(year). Missing years use the existing within-series filling policy. Premise's no-deterioration policy and default maximum index of 1.25 remain unchanged. Missing/invalid proxy references remain explicitly unavailable; no regional curve is borrowed.

The ratio scales each original Large car's energy consumption. It does not replace its absolute consumption, mass or other baseline specifications with SUV values. The existing future battery accounting then responds to the corrected energy trend, retaining that car's own electric range. Fleet-volume aliases and exchanges are unchanged, so the donor fleet is not counted twice.

Array attributes, structured vehicle logs and dataset comments distinguish proxy intensity from direct intensity and record the actual variable, region, 2020 reference and reason. A native signal replaces the proxy whenever available. Repeated source recording does not duplicate the comment.

Each of the four restored REMIND cases has 40 proxy region/technology pairs: five powertrains in CHA, EUR, IND, JPN, MEA, NEU, REF and SSA. The 25 native Large Car pairs in CAZ, LAM, OAS, USA and World retain their original trends. The REMIND-EU case has 100 proxy pairs and 25 native Large Car pairs. These are conditional mappings, not a hard-coded list of regions.

Controlled results

Full builds use encrypted REMIND SSP2-NPi and ecoinvent 3.12 cutoff, compared with the preceding BEV-truck payload correction. No workbook or encrypted scenario file changed. No persistent user Brightway database was written.

All exchange differences are independently explained. The 2050 changes concern 40 Large car services and their 16 BEV/PHEV production suppliers. They follow operating energy, fuel-derived emissions, hydrogen compression, battery capacity and the established mass-dependent maintenance/road/wear relationships. Fleet-market coefficients, all truck inventories and unrelated exchanges stay unchanged. Vehicle manufacture per kilometre is not scaled by fuel efficiency.

For EUR, the effective 2050 energy multiplier is now 0.8 instead of 1 for all five affected mappings. The Large BEV battery becomes 77.952 kWh rather than 97.440 kWh while retaining its 466.17 km electric range. Its pack mass is 428.31 kg at the approved future cell density. The gasoline PHEV becomes 33.869 kWh while retaining its 194.19 km electric-only range. These are accounting projections using the existing baseline designs, not independent engineering forecasts or identical Large-car/SUV hardware assumptions.

The eight additional warnings are CO hot-reference screens for the gasoline Large car in the eight proxy regions. The CO inventory is unchanged. Reduced fuel use lowers the diagnostic's energy-based hot-reference denominator. For EUR the inventory remains 0.000890741 kg/km; the new reference is 0.000395371 kg/km, moving the ratio from about 1.80 to 2.25. This crosses the existing heuristic factor-of-two screen; it is not an increase in emissions or evidence of a EURO-limit violation. warning_changes.json records each diagnostic and explanation. No emission factor was clipped to suppress it.

Tests and reproducibility

291 focused tests pass. New regressions cover native direct and energy/service priority, same-region isolation, 2020 normalization, invalid/missing proxy data, units, gap filling, monotonicity, unchanged fleet aliases, powertrain matching and truthful source metadata. Ten real-file model/family checks preserve existing IMAGE, TIAM-UCL, MESSAGE, GCAM and WITCH calculations; this does not resolve their previously recorded source-data gaps.

The four REMIND cases and one REMIND-EU case verify 4,160 proxy year cells against independent NumPy calculations. All other 3,590 region/technology curves retain their prior values. proxy_factors.csv contains the 260 proxy pairs and normalized 2030/2050 factors. iam_verification.json records the coverage, input hashes and cross-model results. The two build_*.json files record source, mapping, workbook and encrypted-scenario hashes. The full-build audit checks those hashes, every exchange, range and final fuel balances.
