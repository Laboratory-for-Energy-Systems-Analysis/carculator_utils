Scope and boundary
------------------
The two liner-specific 700-bar tank recipes are normalized to one kg of dry
assembly, including tank balance of plant and excluding stored hydrogen.
Their listed materials sum to 87.71 kg (HDPE) and 113.169 kg (aluminium).
The paper has inconsistent nominal masses and scenario labels; this is an
explicit dry-mass boundary choice, not evidence identifying a missing material.
Material proportions and existing supplier proxies are preserved.
Manufacturing electricity is converted from MJ to kWh. Processing services
remain separate from material mass. Tank sizing and fuel-cell efficiency
parameters are unchanged.

exchange_changes.csv contains every changed exchange (positive purchase
amounts). Electricity source quantities are MJ per assembly; the other source
quantities are kg per assembly or kg processed per assembly. Corrected energy
purchases are kWh per kg of dry assembly.

Verification
------------
The focused carculator tests cover material/energy balance, build idempotence,
upstream recipe guards, untouched matrix columns, aligned bundle hashes,
zero foreground coefficients in B, and completed car/bus/truck FCEVs.
Both tank options export with exactly one kg of materials and a source/boundary
comment; export preserves the caller's matrix.
The model comparison uses a 2025 Medium FCEV, GLO, WLTC, default fuel and input
parameters, 200000 km, EF midpoint climate change and SSP2-NPi. The vehicle GWP
difference equals the change in tank unit burden times tank mass / lifetime.
See report.json. This is not a regenerated publication figure.

Reproduce the model/export comparison from the carculator_utils root:

  python dev/validate_hydrogen_tanks.py --old-a /tmp/legacy-tank-A.npz --output /tmp/tank-validation

committing, use the pre-correction revision. The old A SHA256 is recorded in
report.json and the build manifest.
