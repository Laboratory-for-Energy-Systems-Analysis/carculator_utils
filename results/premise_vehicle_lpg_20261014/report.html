LPG vehicle fallback and coverage

The medium LPG car and four LPG trucks now explicitly record their actual fuel supplier, approved CNG proxy and scenario-coverage limits. The previous generic truck comment incorrectly implied regional scenario fuel supply for these services. Their inventory quantities were already consistent with the approved fossil LPG policy; this correction changes metadata and validation.

Supply and model boundary

In REMIND SSP2-NPi, all five RER services purchase market for liquefied petroleum gas from Europe without Switzerland, in kilograms, in both 2020 and 2050. They retain the background supplier; they do not purchase an EUR scenario LPG blend. There is no mapped REMIND LPG vehicle efficiency trend. Their 2020 operating energy remains unchanged in 2050.

Fuel demand preserves the CNG proxy's modelled energy using the approved LPG lower heating value of 45.5 MJ/kg. Tailpipe CO2 uses 0.0631 kg/MJ, or 2.87105 kg/kg LPG, with zero biogenic CO2 for this fossil fallback. This is the existing full-oxidation convention, not a new elemental-carbon balance including separately reported CO, methane and NMVOC.

CNG manufacture, including its tank, efficiency, maintenance and non-CO2 combustion factors remain documented proxies. The LPG services retain their existing compressed-gas vehicle-production links. CNG pump-to-tank methane leakage stays excluded, while combustion methane in the driving compartments is retained. No new LPG engineering model or tank resizing is introduced.

Conditional support for other models

The fallback does not disable explicit LPG pathways in other IAMs. TIAM-UCL already declares LPG vehicle efficiency mappings. Those mappings can still apply their own recorded efficiency factor. Valid properties of a linked fuel supplier take priority over the fossil fallback and determine energy and fossil/non-fossil CO2. Fuel-blend coverage and vehicle-efficiency coverage are reported separately; one does not establish the other.

Focused car and truck tests exercise TIAM-UCL mappings with an illustrative 0.8 energy multiplier and a mixed-origin supplier. These are regression fixtures, not predictions of TIAM-UCL fuel composition or full TIAM-UCL builds. The complete database builds in this step cover REMIND SSP2-NPi only.

Implementation and checks

The generation adapters attach vehicle lpg policy to the five service templates. It records the 2020 fuel-energy baseline, functional unit, original model, proxy boundaries and exact IAM technology. The installer adds these records and corrects the comments in lci-pass_cars.xlsx and lci-trucks.xlsx. All other workbook fields, exchange quantities and the 289 activity identities are preserved, verified by parsing both workbooks before and after installation. Backups and hashes identify the previous files.

After final fuel linking, efficiency adjustment and the existing fuel-module CO2 calculation, Premise records premise vehicle LPG supply and a matching comment. It identifies the actual supplier and geography, delivered energy basis, carbon origin, applied efficiency factor and mapping availability. Repeating this metadata update leaves the record and comment unchanged.

Validation re-examines the current exchanges and suppliers. It rejects accidental CNG substitution, wrong fuel units, missing suppliers, unsupported energy changes, mismatched fossil/non-fossil CO2 and reintroduced CNG leakage. It also prevents an LPG market name with a non-LPG product from qualifying for the fuel module's fossil exception. Forwarding activities are excluded from direct-fuel accounting. Existing fuel-module carbon logic is reused.

The expanded suite passes 314 tests, including 23 new LPG cases and the actual Brightway Excel importer. The cases cover compact exchanges, non-unit reference production, repeatability, deliberately corrupted final exchanges, supplier properties, conditional TIAM-UCL mappings and installed metadata.

Full builds use encrypted REMIND SSP2-NPi and ecoinvent 3.12 cutoff, compared with the immediately preceding Large Car efficiency correction. Both report zero validation errors. Warning identities and counts are unchanged: 401 in 2020 and 468 in 2050. The five LPG services and their vehicle-production coefficients are exactly unchanged. The whole-inventory comparison finds no coefficient differences beyond floating-point precision, using relative tolerance 1e-12 and absolute tolerance 1e-18; the verification summary records the small background differences explicitly. Activity identities, IAM files, transport mappings and fuel-property definitions are preserved. No persistent user Brightway database was written and no new LCIA comparison is needed for this metadata-only inventory correction.

Reproducibility and remaining work

installation.json records the workbook and generator hashes. build_2020.json and build_2050.json record the complete build inputs, source hashes and private inventory hashes. lpg_coverage.csv contains all ten year/template checks. verification_summary.json and verification_sources.json record the independent inventory comparison, tests and audit provenance. The private inventory and full validation dumps are excluded from version control. Premise's implementation, regression tests, documentation and workbook metadata are committed as 7083f17.

Reproduction uses Python 3.12 and the existing isolated source-database copy:

/private/tmp/premise-metals-venv/bin/python dev/build_premise_vehicle_battery.py \
  --year 2050 --workspace /private/tmp/premise-vehicle-lpg-20261014 \
  --output results/premise_vehicle_lpg_20261014 \
  --inventory-installation results/premise_vehicle_lpg_20261014/installation.json
/private/tmp/premise-metals-venv/bin/python dev/audit_premise_vehicle_lpg.py

Repeat the build command for 2020 in a fresh output/workspace before auditing; the script refuses to overwrite a recorded build. The audit also needs the private preceding Large Car builds. The metadata installer is for the recorded pre-update workbook hashes and refuses to repeat an already recorded install.

The next planned issue is general dataset provenance: model/publication references, source revisions, workbook headers and metadata persistence through saved Brightway readback. Final physical plausibility and controlled old/new EF 3.1 comparisons remain later steps. Fuel-tank resizing remains skipped at the user's request.
