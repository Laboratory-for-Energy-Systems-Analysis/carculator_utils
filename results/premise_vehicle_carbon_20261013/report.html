Final linked vehicle fuel-carbon accounting: step 3

Completed on 13 October 2026. The existing Premise fuel module already reclassified CO2. This correction keeps that responsibility in the fuel module and applies it at the correct points in the transformation sequence. Each completed 2020/2050 build now passes 495 direct-fuel car/truck carbon checks with no remaining mismatch. The focused suite passes 342 tests.

What was wrong

The liquid/gas transformations ran before vehicle regionalization. Regional clones therefore inherited the template's CO2 split even after purchasing a different region's fuel. The former calculation also used IAM energy shares and reference carbon intensities, which do not fully describe the actual supplier's carbon amounts. Finally, efficiency scaling changed exchange amounts without scaling the recorded CO2 transfer amounts. A later reclassification could reuse those stale quantities.

The preceding step supplied explicit energy and carbon properties for the final fuel suppliers. This step uses those properties; it does not change their recipes, the approved 46.5 MJ/kg CNG boundary or vehicle fuel purchases.

Implementation and accounting boundary

  • premise/fuels/carbon.py owns the common vehicle calculation and reuses reclassify_fuel_co2. The older liquid/gas calls still relink vehicles, but defer their exhaust calculation to this common routine.

  • Fuel transformation calls the helper after final fuel linking and CNG property annotation. Transport calls it after regionalization, efficiency changes and final transport linking. Each fraction equals the sum of actual fuel purchases times the linked supplier's corresponding CO2 factor.

  • The vehicle positive exhaust pool uses the agreed full-oxidation convention. Existing vehicle biogenic exhaust is replaced along with fossil exhaust, rather than retained as an extra source unrelated to the final fuel blend. Source air compartments are retained, with the existing documented fallback where an exact counterpart flow is unavailable.

  • rescale_exchange scales stored transfer amounts with the exchange. Reclassification's internal amount setter opts out, since that operation must not rescale other fuel transfers. Failed target resolution is atomic for the vehicle update; repeat execution leaves corrected data unchanged.

  • premise vehicle fuel CO2 metadata record actual providers, units, quantities, oxidation factors, reference production and calculated amounts. Unknown carbon origins remain diagnostic. The approved fossil LPG exception uses 45.5 MJ/kg and 0.0631 kg CO2/MJ and records the actual supplier geography.

  • Car/truck validation independently recomputes both fractions from current links. A correct total cannot hide an incorrect fossil/non-fossil split.

Industrial/CCS consumers retain the existing residual-emissions treatment. Their capture inputs and negative credits are not replaced by the vehicle full-oxidation calculation. CO, CH4, NMVOC and other pollutants are unchanged; these checks do not establish elemental carbon closure. Atmospheric carbon from DAC is represented as non-fossil, without implying biomass origin. Capture-origin factors retain the assumptions documented in step 2.

Full scenario checks

Both builds use REMIND SSP2-NPi and ecoinvent 3.12 cutoff, with the same current Premise source, IAM files and vehicle workbooks. Each year is compared with its saved step-2 build. There are 42,564 activities in 2020 and 42,574 in 2050. The 495 checks per year comprise 273 passenger-car and 222 truck services across 17 locations, including all 13 REMIND regions and retained original geographies. This includes legacy background road services as well as refreshed templates. It does not restore historical classes to the refreshed catalogue. Forwarders and vehicles without direct supported combustion fuel are excluded from the direct-fuel count.

Mismatch counts use relative tolerance 1e-8 and absolute tolerance 1e-12 kg for each CO2 fraction. Some differences are small; a mismatch count is not a count of material LCIA changes. Warning identities and multiplicities match the preceding builds exactly. These inherited warnings remain recorded and are not claimed to be resolved by this correction.

The independent audit sums final quantities and provider factors, checks both emission fractions and the transfer ledger, and verifies exact repeatability of the dedicated CO2 updater on all 495 services in each completed inventory. All activity identities are retained. Across the entire database, the only numerical exchange changes are car/truck CO2. Fuel purchases, other pollutants, manufacture and hardware are unchanged. The comparison tolerance outside CO2 is relative 1e-11 and absolute 1e-18. The repeatability claim covers this carbon updater, not every sector's complete transformation or fleet-market creation.

For the MEA 40 t diesel truck, fossil CO2 changes from 0.0308948 to 0.0710593 kg/tkm, while non-fossil CO2 falls from 0.0401648 to 0.0000002540 kg/tkm. Its total and fuel input are unchanged. The previous 56.52% share came from the EUR template, whereas the actual MEA supplier is almost entirely fossil. The USA CNG example similarly removes an inherited European fraction; it does not increase the total carbon emitted.

Total positive CO2 changes range from -11.7005% to +5.03285% in 2020 and -11.7134% to +5.06630% in 2050 across the full checked catalogue. Changes in total are not described as mere reclassification. They reconcile original exhaust amounts with the explicit final-fuel oxidation convention.

The largest decrease is a legacy Brazilian 16--32 t EURO 5 diesel service. In 2020 it retained 0.009643284 kg/tkm of original biogenic exhaust in addition to a new 0.009619499 kg/tkm tracked fuel transfer. Its recorded non-fossil exhaust was consequently 0.019262783 kg/tkm, whereas its actual fuel purchase implies 0.009638432 kg/tkm. The updated vehicle calculation replaces the whole exhaust pool, eliminating this inherited extra amount. Total CO2 falls from 0.08873236 to 0.07835023 kg/tkm. Smaller changes in refreshed EUR templates reflect fuel-specific oxidation factors rather than an assumed common factor for every blend component. Per-service figures are in the comparison CSV.

Regression evidence

The expanded focused suite passes 342 tests, with three existing dependency or deprecation warnings. Eighteen new cases cover regional suppliers, pure fossil and pure non-fossil endpoints, compact exchanges, efficiency scaling, mixed-fuel PHEVs, reference production, fossil LPG fallback, unresolved origin, forwarders, atomic failure, uncertainty, validation and the transport entry point for cars/trucks in 2020/2050. Existing industry/CCS tests remain green.

Two temporary runtime mutations demonstrate that the regressions are detected: disabling the post-transport refresh fails all four regional integration cases; disabling transfer scaling fails both scaling cases. Neither mutation was written to the source tree. Logs are retained with this report.

Reproduction and retained artifacts

The sibling Premise checkout contains the production fix and tests. From this carculator_utils checkout, with the documented Python 3.12 environment:

python dev/build_premise_vehicle_carbon.py --year 2020
python dev/build_premise_vehicle_carbon.py --year 2050
python dev/audit_premise_vehicle_carbon.py
python dev/report_premise_vehicle_carbon.py

The build script deliberately refuses to overwrite a completed build record. It requires the private ecoinvent source project and authorized IAM access; complete background inventories remain outside the repository. Persistent user Brightway databases were not written. The aggregate artifacts are:

  • build_2020.json / build_2050.json: source, workbook, IAM and private inventory hashes, versions, execution time and validation counts.

  • vehicle_carbon_comparison.csv: all 990 before/after service checks.

  • changed_CO2_exchanges.csv: changed direct CO2 coefficients by compartment.

  • transfer_bookkeeping.csv: stored transfers and emitted non-fossil CO2.

  • verification_summary.json and validation_*.json: independent checks and complete validation reports.

  • tests.log and mutation_*.log: focused and regression-detection evidence.
