Direct vehicle energy intensities with cross-model compatibility

The REMIND car/truck intensity correction is implemented, tested and installed in the four local encrypted scenarios. Other IAMs retain their existing efficiency calculation. The controlled 2020 database has no material inventory changes. The 2050 differences are fully explained by recovered energy trends and a separately corrected vehicle-manufacturing selection bug.

Implemented changes

energy_intensity_aliases are optional, model-specific mappings. The 30 REMIND car entries and 20 truck entries now also name their direct Energy intensity series, for both REMIND and REMIND-EU. All existing fleet-volume, energy-use and fuel mappings were checked to be unchanged.

For direct series, the efficiency index is intensity(2020) / intensity(year). The direct input requires MJ/vehicle-km units and a finite positive 2020 value. It can be used even if 2020 fleet activity is zero. Existing monotonicity rules and the default 1.25 maximum efficiency index remain in force. The inverse is the multiplier applied to vehicle energy purchases, with a minimum of 0.8 under that cap.

Missing direct inputs can use an available energy/service pair from the same technology and region. Otherwise they return a neutral factor explicitly marked unavailable. Direct-intensity processing never borrows another technology's or region's curve. Array attributes and each regionalized vehicle's log parameters / IAM efficiency source record the source. The separate EUR Large Car class remains unavailable: no SUV fleet volume or intensity was assigned without an agreed proxy rule.

Four locally installed encrypted files each gained 545 mapped rows: SSP1-PkBudg1000, SSP2-NPi, SSP2-PkBudg1000 and SSP3-rollBack. Every original plaintext byte is retained; only missing intensity rows are appended. The restoration helper checks model, scenario, units, unique identities and the existing transport values against the matching 3.5.2 original. It rejects another scenario version and conflicting existing data. Reapplying it is idempotent. Encrypted backups and candidates remain in the private workspace.

The source filtering notebook's whitelist now includes Energy intensity. Only that source-list entry was added. JSON and cell syntax were checked, the whitelist was evaluated in isolation, and unrelated cells/outputs were verified unchanged. The complete export notebook was not executed: it currently points at IMAGE files and would write unrelated exports. This change and its before/ after hashes are in installation.json. No remote scenario archive was republished; future downloads require the same data preparation for direct signals, with the documented fallback available for older archives.

Operating energy must not scale vehicle manufacture

The database comparison exposed an inherited bug in the substring fuel filter: diesel also matched lorry production, diesel. This reduced the number of trucks manufactured per tonne-kilometre when applying an efficiency trend.

The car/truck selector now requires an energy product and its appropriate unit, in addition to the IAM fuel mapping. Fuel-labelled trucks, engines, fuel cells and electricity meters do not qualify. Direct energy suppliers and market groups remain supported. Regression fixtures include vehicle manufacture and diesel-engine purchases, which must remain unchanged when operating energy is scaled.

In the final 2050 build, 62 previously scaled manufacturing exchanges return to their 2020 per-service quantities, each 25% above the incorrectly reduced value. The number of units inside each vehicle-production inventory, nominal battery capacities, replacement counts, physical masses and other hardware specifications are unchanged by this correction. Coupled constant-range hardware sizing remains a subsequent task.

Remaining boundaries

The recovered direct series describe fleet-level technical energy trends, not specifications for newly manufactured vehicles. A hybrid's total intensity does not separately establish its electric/combustion mode engineering inputs. Constant-range hardware calibration, the EUR Large Car proxy and unmapped technologies remain explicit follow-up decisions.

Compatibility preserves the other models' existing efficiency calculations; it does not certify all their input data. The inspected MESSAGE file has no detailed car/truck mappings. Fourteen IMAGE truck fleet entries lack an energy alias. One TIAM-UCL car energy alias and two GCAM truck aliases are absent from the inspected files. The WITCH source has missing car aliases and reports tkm for mapped car services and pkm for mapped truck services, requiring a separate source/unit review. None of these gaps was filled with an invented improvement.
